# Spores -- image prompts (Battle for Earth, verb: INFECT)

Matching the Grey and Reptilian sheets. Paste the STYLE block first, then one class line.

STYLE (every prompt):
Stylized 3D game character render, full body, standing in a relaxed A-pose facing camera, flat saturated blue studio
backdrop, soft studio lighting with a warm rim. Chunky proportions, big readable silhouette, scrappy street-punk alien
gear: rusty bolted metal, worn leather straps, cloth rags, mismatched sneakers or wrapped boots. The species is walking
FUNGUS: mushroom-cap heads, gill frills under the cap, spongy pale-violet and acid-green skin with pores and freckles,
glowing acid-green spore sacs and veins, little drifting spore puffs. Team paint is pure CRIMSON cloth (sash, armband,
banner rag) carrying the Spore emblem: a cracked pod bursting with spores. No text, no weapons floating, one character.

CLASSES:
1. WORKER (Sproutling) -- small, round, button-mushroom cap with a bite out of it, oversized gardening gloves, a
   backpack of glowing spore jars, a crimson bandana. Cheerful, a little feral.
2. GRUNT (Spikers) -- medium, lean, shaggy-mane mushroom cap, spines growing out of shoulders and forearms, crimson
   armband, scrap shin guards, holds a thorny club grown from wood. Pack fighter.
3. SHOOTER (Puffers) -- medium, puffball head with a ring of vents, a long scrap spore-rifle fed by a glass canister of
   green gas on the hip, gas-mask filter at the mouth, crimson sash.
4. BRUTE (Bloaters) -- big and heavy, bloated translucent belly sac sloshing with glowing goo, thick stubby legs,
   bracket-fungus shoulder plates, riveted belly strap, crimson loincloth rag. Walks into fights to burst.
5. FAST (Root Crawler) -- low, wide, four-legged crouch, morel-cap head, long root-tendril legs ending in hooked toes,
   riding harness and tiny crimson pennant on its back. Skitters.
6. HEAVY (Spore Cannon) -- huge walking mushroom beast with a tall stalk-cannon growing from its back (a puffball barrel
   with crimson bands), a small Spore gunner riding the saddle, iron harness and chains.
7. HERO (The Mother Cap) -- tall and regal, enormous purple-spotted cap like a crown with crimson spots, flowing gill
   cape, glowing veined staff of living mycelium, spores spiralling round her. Calm, terrifying.
8. LOCAL (extra, like the Grey DJ) -- a lazy teenage spore with a sideways cap, headphones, glow-stick spores, crimson
   hoodie. Not a soldier; a vibe.
9. LINEUP -- all of the above standing side by side at true relative size on the same blue backdrop, labelled under
   each: WORKER, GRUNT, SHOOTER, BRUTE, FAST, HEAVY, HERO, LOCAL.
